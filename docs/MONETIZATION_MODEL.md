- Alternative interpretations of evidence
- What they might not see about themselves

**The connection**
- How their natural ability connects to demonstrated value

- Evidence that others recognize this

**Where this creates value**
- Specific contexts where this matters
- Who needs this
- Why they pay for it

### Section 3: Your Unique Positioning

**Current picture**
- Natural / Practical / Measurable scores explained
- Why the scores landed where they did
- What's strong and what's underdeveloped

**Why the scores are what they are**
- Evidence supporting each score
- Where positioning is weak vs. strong
- What's preventing higher scores

**What would strengthen this?**

Experiments to run
- Specific, concrete things to test
- How to validate the positioning
- Ways to gather more evidence

Evidence to collect
- What signals would strengthen positioning
- How to document outcomes
- What data matters

Questions to ask yourself
- Reflection prompts specific to their Architecture
- Challenges to their assumptions
- Exploration prompts

**It's not generic career advice**
- Every section connects to their specific answers
- Quotes come from their responses
- Questions are about their Architecture

**It's generative, not prescriptive**

- Asks "what if" not "you should"
- Suggests experiments, not paths

- Feels like it was written for them

- Uses their evidence to explore
