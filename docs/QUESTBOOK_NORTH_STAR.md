# QUESTBOOK NORTH STAR

**Status:** Locked Product Vision  
**Last Updated:** August 2026  
**Purpose:** Prevent scope drift and guide all product decisions  

---

## THE ONE-SENTENCE VISION

**Questbook helps you see yourself clearly enough to choose where you want to go.**

Not: Get you to a specific destination.  
Not: Turn you into an entrepreneur or consultant.  
Not: Build your business model.  

Instead: **Help you understand yourself so you can make better decisions.**

---

## THE CORE PRINCIPLE: REVEAL, NOT PRESCRIBE

This principle governs everything—language, algorithm, UX, recommendations, scoring, design.

### ❌ Don't Do This
```
"You are a consultant."
"You should start a business."
"Your career path is marketing."
"Build this offer."
"Target this audience."
```

### ✅ Do This Instead
```
"Here are patterns we're seeing in you."
"Here are possibilities worth exploring."
"You decide what they mean."
"What experiments could test this?"
"What matters most to you?"
```

**The difference:** User owns interpretation. Questbook provides evidence.

---

## THE ONE RING & THE SEVEN RINGS

**One Ring**
- Your emerging centre of gravity
- The strongest/core capability or combination
- What makes you distinctive
- Revealed by Module 1
- The gravitational centre of your architecture

**Seven Rings**
- The wider ecosystem that makes you you
- Talents, interests, experiences, values, abilities, contradictions, curiosities
- What makes you complex and powerful
- Revealed by Module 2
- Often where magic happens (intersections, unexpected combinations)

**The relationship:** The One Ring gives you focus. The Seven Rings give you depth.

Most people only see fragments. Questbook helps you see the whole system.

## THE PROGRESSION (NOT A FUNNEL)

```
MODULE 1: SELF
"Who am I?"
Evidence-based discovery of core capabilities
Output: One Ring emerges

↓ (Deeper layer)

MODULE 2: CAPABILITY
"What else is in me?"
Discovery of the wider ecosystem
Output: Seven Rings revealed

↓ (Deeper layer)

MODULE 3: POSSIBILITY
"What could I become?"
Exploration of futures (not prescription)
Output: Expanded sense of possibility

↓ (Deeper layer)

MODULE 4: CONTEXT
"Where do I thrive?"
Finding fit in environments, problems, people
Output: Clarity on where your architecture creates value

↓ (Deeper layer)

MODULE 5: CREATION
"What can I create?"
Real-world experiments and testing
Output: Lived experience, not just understanding

(VALUE & EVOLUTION emerge naturally as you live)
```

This is a **spiral of deepening understanding**, not a funnel toward a predetermined outcome.

Each module helps you see yourself more clearly.

What you do with that understanding is **yours to decide**.

---

## THE PROGRESSION IS NOT BUSINESS-FOCUSED

**NOT this:**
```
SELF → SKILLS → OFFER → AUDIENCE → BUSINESS
(Linear funneling toward entrepreneurship)
```

**Instead this:**
```
SELF → CAPABILITY → POSSIBILITY → CONTEXT → CREATION
(Expanding circles of understanding)
```

Possible applications of self-understanding:
- Start a business ✓
- Change careers ✓
- Create art ✓
- Find meaningful work ✓
- Mentor others ✓
- Deepen relationships ✓
- Understand yourself better ✓
- Change nothing; live more intentionally ✓
- Build a community ✓
- Write something ✓
- Make better decisions ✓

All equally valid.

**Questbook's job:** Help you see clearly.

**Your job:** Decide what to do with that clarity.

## THE LIVING WORKBOOK PRINCIPLE

Questbook is not static.

Your architecture evolves as you live, learn, and experience.

```
QUESTBOOK V01 (2024)
"Here's who I understand myself to be"
↓ (Time, experience, learning)
QUESTBOOK V02 (2025)
"I've discovered this. My understanding has shifted."
↓ (Time, experience, learning)
QUESTBOOK V03 (2026)
"I can see the pattern now. I'm becoming..."
```

The user should eventually be able to look back and see their evolution.

**This is the product:** Not a one-time assessment, but a personal book that gets deeper every time you return to it.

## WHAT QUESTBOOK IS

- An interactive workbook
- A self-discovery system
- A personal exploration tool
- A progressive journey
- A living personal document
- A collection of quests
- A way of turning reflection into understanding
- A system for revealing patterns you can't easily see alone
- A mirror that reflects back what's actually there (not what you expect)

---

## WHAT QUESTBOOK IS NOT

**Do not build:**
- A business-building course
- A startup accelerator
- A marketing masterclass
- A personality typing system
- A generic productivity app
- A career counseling tool
- A collection of unrelated worksheets
- A prescriptive guide to your future

**Do not assume every module should:**
- Lead toward business
- Recommend a specific path
- Categorize you into a box
- Teach generic tactics
- Push you toward monetization

---

## LANGUAGE THAT GOVERNS THE PRODUCT

**Prefer:**
- Quest, Chapter, Ring, Discovery
- Pattern, Clue, Map, Architecture
- Skill, Strength, Capability, Possibility
- Exploration, Experiment, Version, Journey
- "Here's what we're seeing..."
- "Worth exploring..."
- "You might notice..."
- "Possibilities include..."

**Avoid:**
- Onboarding flow, Assessment funnel
- Customer acquisition, Conversion journey
- Business module, Lead generation
- "You are...", "You should..."
- "The algorithm determined..."
- "Your career is..."

The language shapes the experience.

Say "discovery" and users feel like explorers.  
Say "assessment" and they feel categorized.

## HOW TO EVALUATE ANY FUTURE FEATURE

Before building anything, ask:

**Does this help the user understand themselves?**
- If yes → potentially relevant

**Does it reveal a pattern they couldn't easily see alone?**
- If yes → strongly relevant

**Does it help them explore a possibility?**
- If yes → potentially relevant

**Does it help them test something in reality?**
- If yes → potentially relevant

**Is it primarily teaching generic tactics or prescribing an outcome?**
- If yes → it probably doesn't belong in core Questbook

## WHAT EACH MODULE SHOULD DELIVER

### MODULE 1: The Architecture of You ✅
User leaves understanding:
- What they naturally do well
- What problems they tend to solve
- What patterns appear repeatedly
- What energizes vs. drains them
- What's recognized by others
- Which skills have evidence
- Their emerging One Ring

**Output:** Clear sense of core self

---

### MODULE 2: The Seven Rings [NEXT]
User leaves understanding:
- What other talents exist in their ecosystem
- What abilities they haven't fully explored
- What contradictions are actually strengths
- What interests keep tugging at them
- What unexpected combinations are possible
- How the pieces relate to each other
- The fuller complexity of who they are

**Output:** Richer, more nuanced self-portrait

---

### MODULE 3: Possibilities [FUTURE]
User leaves understanding:
- What kinds of futures could unfold from this architecture
- What paths exist (not which to take)
- What becomes possible when you see yourself clearly
- What you're actually capable of exploring
- Where this architecture could lead

**Output:** Expanded sense of possibility (not prescription)

---

### MODULE 4: Context [FUTURE]
User leaves understanding:
- Where environments align with their architecture
- What problems pull naturally on their strengths
- Who they work best with
- What contexts energize them
- Where this architecture creates value
- The difference between fit and force

**Output:** Clarity on resonance and belonging

---

### MODULE 5: Creation [FUTURE]
User leaves understanding:
- What experiments test their understanding
- What can only be learned by doing
- How to create from clarity (not desperation)

- What matters most when you try

## VALUE & EVOLUTION (EMERGE NATURALLY)

**Value** doesn't need a dedicated module.

It emerges when someone takes what they've discovered and puts it into the world:
- Someone recognizes their strength and pays for it
- Others seek them out for what they're good at
- They solve problems people care about
- They create things others want
- They contribute in ways that matter

The user doesn't chase value.  
Value naturally flows from clear self-understanding + real action.

**Evolution** happens continuously:
- Each version reveals growth
- Each experiment teaches
- Each application of understanding clarifies
- The book gets deeper the more you live

---

## GUARDRAILS AGAINST SCOPE DRIFT

**When someone proposes a feature, ask:**

- Does this reveal or prescribe?
- Does this deepen understanding or push toward a specific outcome?
- Would we add this if monetization wasn't a factor?
- Does this serve the user's clarity or our business model?
- Is this about discovery or direction?

**If it prescribes, pushes, or presumes the answer—reject it.**

Questbook's competitive advantage is **not** being another business tool.

It's being **genuinely about you**—not disguised sales funnel.

Questbook doesn't get you somewhere specific.

Questbook helps you see yourself clearly enough to **choose** where you want to go.

**UNDERSTAND, NOT CATEGORIZE.**
